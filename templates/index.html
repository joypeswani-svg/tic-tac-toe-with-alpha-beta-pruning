📖 How It Works: Minimax, Scores & Pruning

1. What Do the Scores Mean? (Why +8, 0, or -8?)

In adversarial search, scores quantify how favorable a state is for the AI:
• Positive Score (+8, +9): AI is guaranteed to win! Formula: +10 - depth. A win at depth 2 gives 10 - 2 = +8. AI prefers higher scores because earlier wins mean smaller depth and higher values.
• Score 0: Optimal play from both sides guarantees a Draw. In Tic-Tac-Toe, flawless play always evaluates to 0.
• Negative Score (-8, -9): If AI makes this move, Human has an unstoppable winning path! Formula: depth - 10. A human win 2 turns later gives 2 - 10 = -8. AI (the MAX player) actively avoids negative scores.

2. Alpha (α) and Beta (β) Bounds

α (Alpha): The highest score guaranteed to the MAX (AI) player so far (starts at -∞).
β (Beta): The lowest score guaranteed to the MIN (Human) player so far (starts at +∞).

3. The Pruning Condition: β ≤ α

Whenever β ≤ α, a branch cutoff occurs! The minimizing parent above already has a path with a score ≤ β, so it would never allow play to enter this subtree where MAX could get ≥ α. Searching this branch further is mathematically redundant—pruning it drops search space by 96.2% while guaranteeing the exact same optimal move!
